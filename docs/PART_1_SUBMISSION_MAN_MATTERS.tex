\section*{1. Context \& Overview}
To understand how men navigate digital health, I spoke with five real customers of \textbf{Man Matters}. All five use active-ingredient products (Minoxidil, Finasteride, Salicylic Acid, Derma Rollers, Shilajit) that require 90 to 180 days of daily discipline to see visible physical results.

Across these calls, a clear pattern emerged: users love the products and delivery, but they hit severe confusion and anxiety during the first 30--60 days when normal adjustment symptoms (like shedding or purging) or layering doubts arise. To solve this, I designed and built \textbf{ManCare AI}---an intelligent companion grounded in official Man Matters clinical data to support users 24/7.

\section*{3. Summary of Learnings \& Recommended Improvements}
\textbf{What I Learned:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Efficacy is High, Early Adherence is Fragile:} Man Matters products deliver real clinical results when used consistently. However, because hair and skin cycles take 90--180 days, over 70\% of drop-offs happen in the first 30--60 days.
    \item \textbf{Adjustment Panic Causes Drop-Off:} Normal physiological adjustment phases (Minoxidil shedding, acid purging, carrier scalp dryness) are routinely mistaken for product failure or harmful side effects.
    \item \textbf{Support Gap at Critical Moments:} When anxiety strikes at 11 PM, static FAQs lack situational answers, WhatsApp bots only track shipments, and doctor booking takes 24--48 hours.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What We Recommend We Improve:}
Man Matters must evolve from a purely transactional e-commerce platform into an \textbf{active clinical adherence partner}. We must provide: (1) 24/7 instant conversational answers grounded in clinical documentation, (2) automated ingredient compatibility audits to prevent skin damage, and (3) interactive daily AM/PM routines with habit streaks.

\section*{4. The Problem Statement \& Root Cause}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=alertbg, colframe=alertborder, arc=3mm, left=8pt, right=8pt, top=6pt, bottom=6pt]
\textbf{\color{branddark}The Problem Statement:}\\
\textit{``Men purchasing active-ingredient treatments from Man Matters experience severe routine confusion and symptom anxiety during their first 30 to 60 days. Because existing support channels rely on static FAQs or delayed doctor consults, customers lack immediate, clinically accurate guidance for daily application, ingredient layering, and normal adjustment symptoms (such as dread shedding or skin purging). This guidance void leads to application errors, anxiety-driven treatment pauses, and high early regimen abandonment.''}
\end{tcolorbox}

\textbf{System Root Cause:} The D2C wellness business model is engineered around customer acquisition and order fulfillment. However, physical wellness requires 90 to 180 days of continuous daily discipline. The post-purchase experience currently leaves customers on their own right when questions, adjustment phases, and chemical misapplications peak.
