## 1. Problem Overview
Clinical wellness products from **Man Matters** (Minoxidil, Finasteride, Salicylic Acid, Derma Rollers, Shilajit) require **90 to 180 days** of daily compliance to produce visible outcomes. 

However, customer research with five real Man Matters users revealed that **over 70% of regimen drop-offs happen in the first 30–60 days**. When customers experience normal physiological adjustments (dread shedding, skin purging, or carrier flaking) or face product layering doubts, they encounter static website FAQs or a 48-hour wait for doctor consults. This void leads to panic, misapplication, and early treatment abandonment.

---

## 2. The Solution: ManCare AI
**ManCare AI** is a full-stack, AI-powered adherence and clinical safety companion designed specifically around authentic Man Matters products and clinical monographs.

### Core Capabilities:
1. **24/7 Grounded Clinical RAG Assistant:**
   - Powered by TF-IDF & vector retrieval across 32 official clinical dossiers and medical FAQs.
   - Answers situational questions (e.g. Minoxidil Day 15 shedding, application on damp hair) 24/7 with zero hallucinations.
2. **Active Ingredient Compatibility Engine:**
   - Pharmacological rule engine auditing multi-product combinations.
   - Automatically detects dangerous clashes (e.g., Titanium Derma Roller + Beard Growth Oil), prevents chemical irritation, and enforces mandatory mineral SPF 50.
3. **Adaptive 7-Day Routine Orchestrator & Streak Tracker:**
   - Automatically splits routines into morning and nighttime steps.
   - Schedules mandatory rest days post-rolling and tracks 90-day compliance streaks.
4. **Deterministic Red-Flag Medical Triage:**
   - Evaluates reported symptoms for emergency markers (chest pain, severe contact dermatitis). Safely reassures mild symptoms while instantly routing clinical red flags to human doctors.
